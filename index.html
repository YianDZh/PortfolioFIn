Qualcomm Cluster Power Characterization

Test bench
Load testing
Power distribution

What I did

  • Invalidated a 15 W per-node power budget by instrumenting a Qualcomm QCS6490 single-board computer under staged stress-ng load, logging thermal zones and power rails with a 0.1 s watchdog sampler and capturing >18 W peak draw with ~13% transient rail droop (3.99 V → 3.47 V).
  • Established the 20 W (5 V / 4 A) per-node target adopted for the 24-node V1 power distribution design by validating 12 V → 5 V buck conversion and hand-building the protoboard GPIO power hat used as the test fixture.
